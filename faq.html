Orders & Shipping

Returns & Exchanges

We accept returns within 30 days of delivery, provided the item is unused, unworn and in its original packaging with tags attached. See our Terms & Conditions for full details.

Contact us through the Contact page with your order number and we'll email you a prepaid return label and instructions.

Once we receive and inspect your return, refunds are processed within 5–7 business days back to your original payment method.
