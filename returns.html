CUSTOMER CARE

Returns & Exchanges

We want you to love what you ordered. If something isn't quite right, we've made returns as simple as possible.

Return Policy

  • Returns are accepted within 30 days of delivery
  • Items must be unused, unworn, and in original packaging with tags attached
  • Sale items marked "final sale" are not eligible for return
  • Refunds are issued to your original payment method

How to Start a Return

1

Contact us

Reach out through our Contact page with your order number and which item(s) you'd like to return.

2

Get your label

We'll email you a prepaid return shipping label along with packing instructions.

3

Ship it back

Pack the item securely with the original packaging and tags, and drop it off using the provided label.

4

Get refunded

Once we receive and inspect your return, we'll process your refund within 5–7 business days.

Exchanges

Prefer a different size or color instead of a refund? Let us know when you contact us — we'll do our best to arrange an exchange, subject to availability.

Damaged or Incorrect Items

If your order arrives damaged or you received the wrong item, contact us within 7 days of delivery with photos of the issue and we'll arrange a replacement or refund at no extra cost to you.

Questions?

See our FAQ page for more, or contact us directly.
